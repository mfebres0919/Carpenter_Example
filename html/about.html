ABOUT US

More Than a Business. A Family Legacy.

Grainwork Co. was built on a simple belief — that quality craftsmanship and honest work should be accessible to every homeowner. That belief started with one man and grew into a family.

HOW WE STARTED

STARTED WITH A HAMMER

AND A PLAN

Damien Greer started Grainwork Co. in 2016 after spending over two decades working in construction and carpentry across Atlanta. He had built kitchens for other people, flooring for other companies, and custom furniture for clients who did not even know his name. He decided it was time to build something for himself.

What started as a one-man operation out of a pickup truck grew into a full team when his sons Jordan and Caleb joined the business. Jordan brought a background in project management and client relationships. Caleb brought precision woodworking skills that rival his father's. Together, the three of them built Grainwork into what it is today.

We are Black owned, family operated, and deeply rooted in the Atlanta community. Every project we take on carries the Greer name — and we take that seriously.
