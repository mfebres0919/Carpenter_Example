KITCHEN REMODELING

The kitchen is where your family eats, gathers, and makes memories. It deserves to be a space that works as hard as you do and looks beautiful doing it. At Grainwork, we take a full-service approach to kitchen remodeling — that means we handle demo, carpentry, installation, and finishing all under one roof.

OUR PROCESS

We start with a free in-home consultation where we walk your space, listen to your vision, and take measurements. No pressure, no commitment — just an honest conversation about what's possible and what it costs.

Within a few days we send you a detailed proposal with a full breakdown of materials, labor, timeline, and pricing. Everything is transparent — you'll know exactly what you're paying for before we ever pick up a tool.

Once approved, our crew handles everything from demo to final finishing. We keep your home clean, communicate updates throughout, and don't consider the job done until you're completely satisfied with the result.
